GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about our plans.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan as your needs change. Actual changes would need to be supported by your subscription system.

Can I cancel my subscription?

You can design your service to allow cancellation at any time. Connect a billing provider and implement the cancellation workflow to enable this feature.

What happens when I choose a plan?

This demo shows a confirmation message. To accept payments, connect a backend and a payment provider.

Is the yearly plan discounted?

Yes. The example prices use an assumed 20% discount for annual billing, shown as a monthly-equivalent price.
